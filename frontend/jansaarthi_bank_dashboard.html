Responsible-AI Safeguard → Regulatory Mapping

✓
Consent & purpose limitation
Only customer-permitted data categories are used, for the stated purpose, with a visible Consent Center and revoke option.
Maps to: RBI Digital Lending Guidelines — data minimisation & explicit borrower consent
✓
Explainability
Every recommendation — including a suppressed one — carries a plain-language "why" and a signal-weight breakdown.
Maps to: RBI Digital Lending Guidelines — key fact statement & disclosure norms
✓
No-push rule
Aggressive product offers are automatically suppressed when stress signals are high; support/education is offered instead.
Maps to: RBI guidance against unfair/coercive digital lending practices
!
Bias monitoring
Segment-level acceptance is tracked; the 60+ and first-time-digital gap is flagged and under active review this cycle.
Maps to: RBI IT Governance & Digital Personal Data Protection Act — fair processing
✓
Human escalation
High-risk or ambiguous cases can always be routed to a human officer; "talk to a person" is never hidden behind a bot loop.
Maps to: RBI Digital Lending Guidelines — grievance redressal mechanism
This mapping is illustrative for the prototype — a production rollout would go through formal legal/compliance sign-off against the current RBI circulars and the DPDP Act.
